# 1. WhatsApp Sales Scripts

### Context
WhatsApp is the primary sales channel for QuadERP in Ghana. These scripts are for field sales reps who interact with shop owners directly, either after a physical visit, through a referral, or following up after a demo. Each script should be copy-pasted and lightly personalized before sending.

---

### Usage Notes

- **Personalization is key.** Reps should always replace [Owner Name], [Location], [Day], [Time], and other brackets with real details. Generic messages get ignored.
- **Timing matters.** Send cold outreach messages between 9am-11am or 2pm-4pm. Avoid sending during peak shop hours (lunch rush, evening rush).
- **Don't send all messages at once.** For Script C, respect the Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 5 cadence. Pushing faster feels desperate.
- **Voice notes convert.** For warm referrals especially, a voice note feels more personal than text. Encourage reps to use the script as a guide, not read it word-for-word.
- **Track responses.** Log every conversation outcome in the CRM: Demo Booked, Trial Started, Objection (specify which), No Response, Not Interested. This data informs future scripts.
- **If no response after Script C:** Wait 2 weeks, then send a single "checking in" message with a new feature or customer success story. Never send more than 4 follow-ups total.
