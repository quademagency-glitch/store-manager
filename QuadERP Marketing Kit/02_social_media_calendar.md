📚 How to stop your staff from stealing (without being the bad guy):

Most shop owners in Ghana suspect staff theft but don't know how to prove it, or address it without destroying the relationship.

1️⃣ **Install a system.** When staff know transactions are tracked, behaviour changes immediately. The system is the "bad guy," not you.

4️⃣ **Check the cash drawer report.** If the expected cash doesn't match the actual cash, investigate immediately. Small discrepancies add up.

5️⃣ **Use the data, not emotion.** Don't accuse. Show the numbers. "The system shows 5 voids at closing on Thursday. Can you explain?"

QuadERP does steps 1-4 automatically. You just need to have the conversation.
