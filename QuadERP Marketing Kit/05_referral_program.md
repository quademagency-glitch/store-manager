# 5. Referral Program Copy

This referral program leverages QuadERP's strongest growth channel. Word-of-mouth among shop owners who know and trust each other. The program should be simple enough to explain in one WhatsApp message and rewarding enough to motivate active referrals. All copy is ready to deploy across in-app notifications, email, and WhatsApp.

## Terms & Conditions

**Grow Together Referral Program. Terms**

1. **Eligibility.** Any active QuadERP subscriber on a paid plan can participate. Free trial users become eligible once they subscribe to a paid plan.

2. **Reward.** The referrer receives one (1) month free on their current QuadERP plan for each referred friend who subscribes to any paid plan within 60 days of their initial signup. Free months are applied as billing credits on the next invoice cycle.

3. **Referred customer benefit.** Referred customers receive the standard 30-day free trial plus complimentary setup assistance (valued at GHS 1,000). The setup benefit must be redeemed within 14 days of account creation.

4. **No limit.** There is no cap on the number of referrals or free months a referrer can earn. Free months stack and are applied sequentially to future billing periods.

5. **Tracking.** Referrals are tracked via unique referral links. The referred customer must sign up using the referrer's unique link for the referral to be credited. Referrals cannot be applied retroactively.

6. **Abuse.** QuadERP reserves the right to suspend or terminate referral benefits if abuse is detected, including but not limited to: self-referrals, fake accounts, or referrals made using misleading claims about the product.

## Usage Notes

- **In-app placement:** Add the referral CTA in two places: (1) the main navigation sidebar as "Refer & Earn 🎁" and (2) on the dashboard after the user has been active for 14+ days (don't show it during their first 2 weeks. Let them fall in love with the product first).
- **WhatsApp forwarding:** The pre-written message should be accessible from the referral page with a one-tap "Copy to WhatsApp" button. Use the WhatsApp `wa.me` deep link format to pre-fill the message.
- **Referral link format:** Use short, memorable links like `quaderp.app/r/GRACE2024` or `quaderp.app/r/[username]`.
- **Tracking:** Implement UTM parameters on referral links for attribution. Track: referral link clicks, signups from referral, trial-to-paid conversion rate from referred users, and total free months awarded.
- **Announcement timing:** Send the email to all active subscribers who have been on a paid plan for 30+ days. Follow up with an in-app notification 3 days later for anyone who didn't open the email.
- **Field rep integration:** Sales reps should mention the referral program during onboarding: "When you're happy with the system, you can share your referral link with other shop owners you know. You'll both get rewarded."
