# 7. Print Flyer / Poster Copy (2 Versions)

### Context
These flyers are printed in bulk and used by field sales reps during market walks, shop visits, and business association events. They are left at shops, posted on notice boards in market areas, and sometimes inserted into product deliveries from partner suppliers. The design must be scannable in under 10 seconds, a busy shop owner will glance at it while serving a customer.

**Print specs:** A5 size (148mm × 210mm), full color, double-sided optional but designed as single-sided. Printed on 170-250gsm glossy or matte card stock.

### Color & Brand Guidance

- **Background:** Deep navy blue (#1a1a3e) or charcoal (#1e1e2e). Premium, trustworthy feel
- **Headline text:** White, bold, large (at least 24pt). Must be readable from arm's length
- **Accent color:** Coral/orange (#ff6b35) for the free trial badge and checkmarks. Draws the eye
- **Body text:** White or light grey (#e0e0e0) on dark background
- **QR code:** White background square with dark QR. Ensure sufficient contrast for scanning
- **Logo:** Full-color QuadERP logo on dark background, positioned at bottom center
- **Typography:** Use a clean sans-serif font (Inter, Outfit, or DM Sans). Headline should be heavier weight (700-800), body at regular weight (400-500).
- **Overall feel:** Serious but not scary. The headline provokes urgency; the body provides the solution. Premium, not cheap.

## Usage Notes

- **Distribution strategy:**
  - **Flyer A** (problem-focused) works best for cold outreach. Leave it at shops you haven't visited yet. The provocative headline gets attention.
  - **Flyer B** (feature-focused) works best as a leave-behind after a demo or conversation. It reinforces what you showed them and gives them something to reference.
- **QR codes:** Generate QR codes that link to a WhatsApp pre-filled message: `https://wa.me/[NUMBER]?text=Hi!%20I%20saw%20your%20flyer%20and%20I'm%20interested%20in%20QuadERP.` This lets you track leads coming from print materials.
- **Print quantities:** Start with 500 of each per city. Track QR code scans to measure effectiveness. Double down on whichever flyer drives more WhatsApp conversations.
- **Placement:** Besides direct distribution, place flyers at: accounting offices serving SMBs, wholesale suppliers' counters, business association notice boards, and bank/MFI branches where small business owners visit.
- **Seasonal variants:** Create adapted versions for pharmacy conferences, market association meetings, and trade fairs. Add the event name and date to build relevance.
