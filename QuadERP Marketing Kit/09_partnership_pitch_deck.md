# 9. Partnership Pitch Deck Outline

### Context
This deck is used when pitching potential partners who can help distribute QuadERP to their existing networks. The fundamental pitch is: "You already serve these shop owners. We help them run better. Let's work together so your members/customers/clients get more value, and you get a new revenue stream or engagement tool."

Target partners:
- **Business associations:** AGI (Association of Ghana Industries), GNCCI (Ghana National Chamber of Commerce & Industry)
- **Pharmacy associations:** Pharmaceutical Society of Ghana, Pharmacy Council
- **POS hardware resellers:** Companies selling thermal printers, barcode scanners, cash drawers
- **Accounting firms:** Firms serving SMBs with bookkeeping, tax, and advisory services
- **Telecom companies:** MTN Ghana, Vodafone Ghana. Mobile Money integration angle

**Deck format:** 10 slides, 16:9 aspect ratio. Design should be clean, professional, and data-driven. Use QuadERP brand colors.

## Slide 2: The Problem We're Solving

**Title:** Ghana's Retail Sector is Flying Blind

**Key Message:** The majority of Ghana's 2+ million retail businesses operate without any digital management system. Losing money they can't see.

**Bullet Points:**
- Over 85% of Ghanaian retail shops track sales with paper notebooks or nothing at all
- Average annual shrinkage (theft, errors, waste): 3-5% of revenue. GHS 20,000+ per year for a typical shop
- Credit sales go untracked: business owners lose an estimated GHS 3,000-8,000 annually to forgotten debts
- No visibility across branches: multi-location owners rely on phone calls and physical visits to monitor performance

**Speaker Notes:**
"These aren't our estimates. These are patterns we've validated across hundreds of shop visits in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, and Tamale. The average shop owner doesn't know they have a problem until it's too late. They think their business is profitable until they actually count the stock. This is the gap QuadERP fills."

**Key Message:** QuadERP replaces paper, Excel, and disconnected POS machines with one cloud platform.

**Bullet Points:**

- **Inventory Management:** Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts, barcode scanning, multi-location transfers

- **CRM & Loyalty:** Customer profiles, loyalty points, gift cards, SMS/email campaigns
- **Multi-Location:** Centralized dashboard across all branches

**Visual:** Clean feature grid with icons. Highlight "Loss Prevention" with a "No other system has this" badge.

**Speaker Notes:**
"We're not a simple POS terminal. We're a complete retail management platform built from the ground up for Ghanaian businesses. The loss prevention module is our differentiator. It's AI-powered pattern detection that catches theft and errors automatically. No other system available in Ghana offers this."

## Slide 6: Partnership Models

**Title:** Flexible Partnership Structures

**Key Message:** Choose the model that fits your organization.

**Option A. Referral Partnership (Low Commitment)**
- You refer members/clients to QuadERP using a co-branded link
- We handle all sales, onboarding, and support
- You earn a commission on every subscription (15-20% of monthly revenue, ongoing)
- Minimum commitment: share the link, we do the rest

**Option B. Co-Branded Distribution (Medium Commitment)**
- We create a co-branded version of the onboarding experience (e.g., "QuadERP for [Partner] Members")
- Exclusive pricing or benefits for your members/clients
- Joint marketing: co-branded flyers, social media posts, event presence
- Dedicated account manager for partner-referred customers

**Option C. Strategic Alliance (High Commitment)**
- Deep product integration (e.g., embedded MoMo payments, hardware bundling, API integration)
- Joint go-to-market strategy with shared targets and KPIs
- Revenue sharing model tailored to contribution
- Co-development of features specific to partner's use case

**Speaker Notes:**
"We're flexible. If you want to start small, just sharing a link. We can do that. If you want to go big. Co-branded product, joint marketing, revenue sharing. We're ready for that too. We suggest starting with Option A or B, proving the value, and then expanding."

## Slide 8: Go-to-Market Plan

**Title:** How We Launch Together

**Key Message:** We propose a phased 90-day partnership launch.

**Phase 1. Setup (Week 1-2)**
- Sign partnership agreement
- Create co-branded assets (landing page, flyers, email template)
- Brief partner's team on QuadERP (30-minute virtual training)
- Set up tracking for partner referrals

**Phase 2. Soft Launch (Week 3-6)**
- Partner shares QuadERP with a subset of members/clients (e.g., one region or one segment)
- QuadERP provides dedicated onboarding support for referred customers
- Collect feedback and testimonials from early adopters
- Weekly check-ins between QuadERP and partner teams

**Phase 3. Scale (Week 7-12)**
- Full rollout to partner's entire network
- Joint social media campaign and press release
- Partner event presence (booth at conferences, speaking slot at AGM)
- Monthly partnership review meeting with data report

**Speaker Notes:**
"We're not asking you to go all-in on day one. We start small, prove it works with a subset of your members, and then scale based on real results. By week 12, we'll have clear data on what's working and can plan the next 6-12 months together."

## Usage Notes

- **Customize per partner.** Never send a generic deck. Tailor Slide 5 (What's in it for you), Slide 7 (Case study), and the language throughout to the specific partner type.
- **Keep it under 20 minutes.** Present slides 1-7 in 15 minutes. Leave slides 8-10 for the discussion. The goal is conversation, not a monologue.
- **Bring proof.** If possible, show the product live on your phone for 2 minutes between slides 3 and 4. A live demo is worth 10 slides.
- **Leave-behind.** Send the deck as a PDF via WhatsApp after the meeting, along with a one-page partnership summary (extract of slides 5 and 6).
- **Decision-maker mapping.** For associations: target the Executive Director or Programs Lead. For telecom: Merchant Services or SMB division. For accounting firms: Managing Partner. For resellers: Sales Director.
- **Follow-up cadence.** If no response in 5 business days, send a WhatsApp follow-up with one new data point or customer story. If no response after 3 touches, move to quarterly check-in.
