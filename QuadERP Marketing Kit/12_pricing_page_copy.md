## FAQ Section

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I try QuadERP before paying?**
A: Absolutely. Every plan comes with a **30-day free trial** — full access, no limits, no credit card required. You'll have access to every feature so you can see exactly how it works for your business before spending a single cedi.

**Q: What happens when my free trial ends?**
A: If you subscribe, nothing changes — your data, products, and settings all stay exactly as they are. If you choose not to subscribe, your account is paused (not deleted). Your data is kept safe for 90 days. You can come back and resubscribe at any time within that period and everything will be waiting for you.

**Q: Can I switch plans later?**
A: Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. If you start with Single Branch and open a second location, just upgrade to Multi-Branch — all your existing data carries over. No data loss, no downtime.

**Q: Is there a contract or commitment?**
A: No long-term contracts. QuadERP is month-to-month. You can cancel at any time from your account settings. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in.

**Q: What does the setup fee cover?**
A: The one-time setup fee covers personalized onboarding: a QuadERP specialist visits your shop, loads your products into the system, configures your settings, and trains your staff. If you're comfortable setting up on your own (many customers are), you can skip the setup assistance — but the fee ensures you have the option.

**Q: Do I need special hardware?**
A: No. QuadERP works on any device — your existing phone, tablet, or computer. All you need is a web browser and an internet connection. If you want a barcode scanner or receipt printer, they're optional add-ons but absolutely not required.

**Q: Does it work with Mobile Money?**
A: Yes! QuadERP integrates with MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo Money. Your customers can pay via MoMo and every transaction is automatically tracked in your system alongside cash and card payments.

**Q: What kind of support do I get?**
A: All plans include WhatsApp and email support from real humans (not chatbots) during business hours. Our average response time is under 30 minutes. Franchise customers get priority support with a dedicated account manager and guaranteed response times.

## Usage Notes

- **Pricing page layout:** Use a 3-column layout for the tiers on desktop (Single | Multi-Branch | Franchise). On mobile, stack vertically with Multi-Branch at the top (since it's most popular).
- **"Most Popular" treatment:** The Multi-Branch card should be visually elevated — slightly larger, different background color (or border), and a prominent "⭐ Most Popular" badge at the top. This guides the eye and anchors the decision.
- **Monthly vs. annual toggle:** Consider adding an annual billing option with a discount (e.g., pay 10 months, get 12 — save 17%). This increases LTV and reduces churn. Label it as "Save 17% with annual billing."
- **Sticky CTA on mobile:** On mobile devices, keep a "Start Free Trial" button fixed at the bottom of the screen while the user scrolls through the pricing page. Don't make them scroll back up to convert.
- **A/B test the headline.** Test "Stop Losing Money You Can't See" vs. "Your Shop Deserves Better Than a Notebook" vs. "GHS 8/Day for Complete Control of Your Business."
- **Social proof numbers:** Update the customer count badge monthly. Even "Trusted by 50+ shops" is better than no number. As you grow, this becomes increasingly powerful.
- **Localization:** If the pricing page receives significant traffic from non-English-speaking regions, consider a Twi language toggle for key elements (headline, tier descriptions, CTA).
