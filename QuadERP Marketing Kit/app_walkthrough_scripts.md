# QuadERP App Walkthrough Video Scripts

These scripts are designed to be read alongside screen recordings of the app. The tone is simple, step-by-step, and easy to follow — perfect for someone new to the system.

---

## Video 1: Point of Sale (POS) - Making a Sale
**Duration:** ~1 minute

**[VISUAL]**: Start on the main Dashboard.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Welcome to QuadERP! Let's learn how to make a sale. It's super easy.

**[VISUAL]**: Click on "Point of Sale" or "POS" in the side menu.
**[VOICEOVER]**: First, click on "Point of Sale" on the left side of your screen. This is your digital cash register.

**[VISUAL]**: Type "Soap" in the search bar. Click on a product to add it to the cart.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Imagine a customer wants to buy some soap. See this search bar? Just type what they want. Click the product, and boom! It's added to their shopping cart on the right.

**[VISUAL]**: Click on another product to add it. Change the quantity using the + button.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Let's add one more thing. You can click the plus button if they want two of them.

**[VISUAL]**: Click the "Checkout" button. Select "Mobile Money" (MoMo).
**[VOICEOVER]**: Ready to pay? Click the big "Checkout" button. You can choose Cash, Card, or Mobile Money. Let's pick MoMo.

**[VISUAL]**: Click "Confirm Payment". A receipt appears on screen.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Confirm the payment, and you're done! A receipt pops up, and your stock is updated automatically. Easy, right?

## Video 2: Inventory Management - Tracking Stock
**Duration:** ~1 minute

**[VISUAL]**: Start on the main Dashboard.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Now, let's see how we keep track of what's in the shop.

**[VISUAL]**: Click on "Inventory" or "Products" in the side menu.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Click "Inventory" on the left menu. This is your digital stockroom.

**[VISUAL]**: Scroll through the list of products. Point out the "Stock" column.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Here, you can see every item in your shop. Look at the numbers on the right—that tells you exactly how many you have left. No more counting by hand!

**[VISUAL]**: Show a product with a "Low Stock" warning badge (often in yellow or red).
**[VOICEOVER]**: See this red badge? The app is warning you that you're running out of this item. It tells you *before* it finishes, so you can order more.

**[VISUAL]**: Click on a product to view its details. Show the history tab if available.
**[VOICEOVER]**: If you click on an item, you can see its whole story—when you bought it and when you sold it. The app watches your shelves for you!

## Video 3: Loss Prevention - Keeping Money Safe
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

**[VISUAL]**: Start on the Dashboard. Look for an "Alerts" or "Loss Prevention" section.
**[VOICEOVER]**: QuadERP is like a security guard for your money. Let me show you the Loss Prevention feature.

**[VISUAL]**: Click on the Alerts/Loss Prevention menu item. Show a list of flagged activities.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Click here to open the Alerts. The app watches every sale. If something looks strange, it tells you right away.

**[VISUAL]**: Click on a specific alert, like "Suspicious Void".
**[VOICEOVER]**: For example, what if a worker cancels a sale after the customer leaves and pockets the cash? QuadERP flags it. It shows you the time, the item, and who did it.

**[VISUAL]**: Show a stock discrepancy alert.
**[VOICEOVER]**: It also alerts you if stock disappears without a sale. It catches mistakes and stops money from leaking out of your business.

## Video 4: Multi-Branch & Dashboard - The Big Picture
**Duration:** ~1 minute

**[VISUAL]**: Start on the Dashboard.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Finally, let's look at the Dashboard. This is your control center.

**[VISUAL]**: Highlight the "Today's Sales" and "Total Profit" numbers.
**[VOICEOVER]**: Right at the top, you see the big numbers. How much money you made today, and what your actual profit is. It calculates this for you instantly.

**[VISUAL]**: Scroll down to the charts (e.g., Top Selling Products).
**[VOICEOVER]**: You can see beautiful charts showing what your best-selling items are.

**[VISUAL]**: Go to the top corner and click the location dropdown. Switch from "Branch 1" to "All Locations".
**[VOICEOVER]**: Have more than one shop? Look at this. You can click here and see what's happening at Branch 2, Branch 3, or all of them together. You can manage your whole empire from your phone!
